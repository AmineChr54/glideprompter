Welcome to **GlidePrompter**. [PAUSE]<br>
This is a sample script. Every line in the file is one paragraph on the prompter.<br>
Press Space to start scrolling, and Space again to pause. [BREATHE]<br>
Use Shift and the arrow keys to change the speed. Tap the arrow keys to nudge the text, or hold them to glide.<br>
Words in square brackets are stage cues. They show up smaller and in colour, so the speaker never reads them out loud. [SMILE]<br>
[SLIDE 2]<br>
A cue that starts with SLIDE becomes a blue badge, so you know when to change the slide.<br>
You can format words in the editor: **bold**, *italic*, __underlined__, ==highlighted== or {orange}coloured{/}. Select a word and press Ctrl+B, Ctrl+I or Ctrl+U.<br>
Pick the font, weight and letter spacing in the Text section. Every speaker keeps their own look.<br>
The timer can count up, or count down from a target time. It turns {yellow}yellow{/} near the end and {red}red{/} when time is up.<br>
Lost your place? Press Ctrl+F in the Control window, type a few words you just heard, and press Enter. The prompter glides right to them.<br>
Press Ctrl+1 to 9 to switch between speakers. To add your own script, type a name under Speakers and click Add, or drop a .txt file into the scripts folder.<br>
Press ? at any time to see every keyboard shortcut. [PAUSE]<br>
Good luck with your talk!
